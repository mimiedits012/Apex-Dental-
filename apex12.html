Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about installations, servicing, or warranties? Find quick answers below.

How quickly can you install a new dental chair unit?

Standard chair unit installations typically take between 4 to 8 hours once the plumbing and electrical lines are ready. Our engineers handle full setup and testing on the same day.

What is included in an Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)?

Our AMC covers scheduled preventive maintenance checks, fluid & filter replacements, electrical calibration, emergency fault callouts, and discounts on replacement spare parts.

Do your manufactured machines come with a warranty?

Yes! All new dental units and machinery manufactured or supplied by Apex Dental Solution come with a comprehensive warranty on technical and structural components.

Do you provide genuine replacement spare parts?

We stock and supply only genuine OEM spare parts, valves, motors, and electronic boards to ensure your equipment maintains factory-certified performance and safety standards.
